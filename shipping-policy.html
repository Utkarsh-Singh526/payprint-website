Delivery / Service Fulfilment Policy

Last updated: September 2026

PayPrint is a self-service physical printing service. There is no courier shipment for a normal kiosk print order.

Service delivery

After successful payment verification, the requested document is sent to the selected PayPrint kiosk for printing and is collected by the customer at that kiosk.

Delivery time

Printing is normally initiated after payment verification. Actual completion time can vary depending on document size, printer status, queue and network conditions.

Service interruption

If a kiosk is unavailable or a technical issue prevents fulfilment, customers should contact PayPrint support using the real contact information published on the Contact page.
