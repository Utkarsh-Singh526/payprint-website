Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

PayPrint respects the privacy of people using its printing services. This policy explains, at a general level, how information may be handled when a customer uses a PayPrint kiosk.

Information we may process

To provide the printing service, the system may process uploaded documents, printing selections, transaction references and basic technical information required to operate the service.

Document handling

Documents are processed for the purpose of providing the requested printing service. PayPrint is designed to minimize unnecessary retention of customer documents. Operational retention periods may depend on the kiosk configuration and service workflow.

Payments

Payment processing is handled through the payment provider used by PayPrint. PayPrint does not request or store a customer's UPI PIN, card PIN or payment password.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed by the service. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the real PayPrint support contact listed on the Contact page.
