Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Before payment

A customer may leave or cancel an unpaid printing session at any time. No payment is collected for an unpaid session.

After successful payment

If a payment is successfully captured but the kiosk cannot complete the requested print because of a technical or printer issue, the customer should contact PayPrint support with the transaction reference and kiosk details. Refunds, where applicable, will be handled according to the payment provider's settlement/refund process and the circumstances of the failed service.

Duplicate or failed transactions

If a customer believes a payment was duplicated or incorrectly charged, they should contact support with the transaction reference so the transaction can be checked.

Contact

Use the real email or phone number on the Contact page for refund/support requests.
