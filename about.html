ABOUT PAYPRINT

Making everyday document printing easier.

PayPrint is a smart self-service printing kiosk concept designed to make document printing faster and more convenient in places such as colleges, libraries, offices and public service locations.

The problem

Traditional printing often requires manual assistance, transferring files through cables or messaging apps, and manually calculating pages and cost.

Our solution

PayPrint combines QR-based document upload, automatic page counting, digital payment and automated printing into one self-service workflow.

Our workflow

  1. Customer scans the QR code shown on the kiosk.
  2. Customer uploads a supported document from a phone.
  3. The system prepares a preview and calculates the printing cost.
  4. Customer completes digital payment.
  5. The kiosk verifies the payment and prints the document.
